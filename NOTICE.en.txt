6. Software fee and issue grant

The software fee is included in the price of the payment archive and is deducted from that price before the shares are divided. The fee is about 0.2 KAS while the issuer holds an unexpired issue grant. The fee is about 1 KAS when there is no grant, the grant has expired, or https://zip.tuwe.com cannot be reached. The fee is paid to the software-fee address fixed in the program.

The remainder of the price is split by the issuer share and the rights-owner share set by the issuer. Those two shares together are the whole remainder.

An issue grant sets the fee band and lasts 7 days. When the program requests a grant, it sends the issuer address to https://zip.tuwe.com. The wallet file and the wallet password stay on the local computer and are not sent to that service. The price is shown before payment.
